# Pancha Mahapurusha and Lunar Yogas (original summary)

The five Mahapurusha ("great person") yogas arise when one of the five non-
luminary planets — Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn — occupies its own
sign or exaltation while sitting in a quadrant (1, 4, 7, 10) from the ascendant.
Mars gives Ruchaka (courage, leadership), Mercury gives Bhadra (intellect,
communication), Jupiter gives Hamsa (wisdom, ethics), Venus gives Malavya
(comfort, beauty), and Saturn gives Sasa (discipline, authority over others).
Each shapes the personality along the nature of its planet.

Gaja Kesari yoga is present when Jupiter sits in a quadrant from the Moon. In
the stricter (B.V. Raman) reading it also requires that Jupiter is supported by
a benefic and is not debilitated, combust, or in an enemy's sign — only then
does it confer reputation, intelligence, and lasting respect.

Kemadruma is an affliction of isolation: the Moon has no planet in the houses
flanking it (the 2nd and 12th from the Moon) and no companion. It can be
cancelled when a planet occupies a quadrant from the Moon or the Moon is well
aspected, so always check for cancellation before judging it.

Neecha Bhanga Raja yoga is the cancellation of a debilitation: a debilitated
planet whose dispositor or exaltation-lord is strong (in a quadrant from the
lagna or Moon) or associates with it has its weakness reversed into a rise.
