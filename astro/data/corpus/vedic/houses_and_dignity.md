# Bhava Significations and Planetary Dignity (original summary)

Each of the twelve bhavas governs a domain of life. In brief: the 1st is the
self, body, and vitality; the 2nd is wealth, family, and speech; the 3rd is
siblings, courage, and effort; the 4th is mother, home, and inner happiness; the
5th is children, intelligence, and past merit; the 6th is enemies, disease, and
debts; the 7th is spouse and partnership; the 8th is longevity, transformation,
and hidden matters; the 9th is fortune, father, and dharma; the 10th is career
and status; the 11th is gains and aspirations; the 12th is loss, expense, and
liberation. Each house also has a natural significator (karaka) — for example
Jupiter for children (5th), Venus for spouse (7th), and the Sun for the self.

A planet's strength depends on its dignity by sign. Exaltation is the strongest
placement and debilitation the weakest; in between, a planet may sit in its own
sign, its moolatrikona portion, or in the sign of a natural friend, a neutral,
or an enemy. A functional reading then asks what the planet rules from the
ascendant: lords of trines tend to act as benefics, lords of growth-and-conflict
houses as malefics, and a planet ruling both a quadrant and a trine becomes a
yogakaraka.

Ashtakavarga grades each sign by accumulated benefic points (bindus). Signs and
houses with high Sarvashtakavarga totals support the matters of that house;
low totals warn of weakness there. Treat the bindu count as a strength weighting
layered on top of house and planet analysis.
