# Raja Yogas and Dhana Yogas (original summary)

A Raja yoga forms when the strength of a quadrant (kendra: 1, 4, 7, 10) joins
the auspiciousness of a trine (trikona: 1, 5, 9). The classic trigger is an
association — conjunction, mutual aspect, or sign exchange (parivartana) —
between a kendra lord and a trikona lord. A single planet that rules both a
kendra and a trikona is itself a raja-yoga karaka and is among the most useful
planets for the chart.

The result of a raja yoga is rise in status, authority, and recognition, timed
to the dasha and bhukti of the planets that form it. The yoga only fruits when a
participating planet runs its period; an unworked yoga lies dormant.

A Dhana (wealth) yoga forms when the lords of the wealth-giving houses — the 2nd
(accumulated wealth, family) and the 11th (gains, income) — connect with the
lords of the 1st, 5th, or 9th. Chandra-Mangala yoga (Moon conjoined Mars) is a
classic wealth combination acting through enterprise and initiative.

Read these as potentials, not guarantees: strength of the participating planets,
their dignity, and freedom from affliction decide how fully the yoga delivers.
