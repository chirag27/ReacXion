# KP Ruling Planets (original summary)

Ruling Planets are the planets in charge at the moment a question is asked or a
chart is cast. They are read from a chart drawn for the query time and place:
the lord of the weekday (the day lord), and the sign lord, star lord, and sub
lord of both the Moon and the rising sign (Lagna) at that instant.

In practice the Ruling Planets act as a filter. The significators of an event
that also appear among the Ruling Planets are the operative ones, and the dasha
or bhukti running under a Ruling Planet tends to bring matters to a head. A node
(Rahu or Ketu) is included when it tenants the star of one of the Ruling Planets,
since a node acts as an agent for the planet whose star it occupies.

Ruling Planets are most useful for confirmation and timing: they narrow a broad
significator list to a short, decisive set and point to the period in which the
promised event is most likely to occur.
