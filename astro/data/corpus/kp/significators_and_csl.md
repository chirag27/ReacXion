# KP Significators and Cuspal Sub Lords (original summary)

Krishnamurti Paddhati judges an event from the houses that promote it and the
sub lord that governs the deciding cusp. House placement is cuspal: a planet
belongs to the bhava whose Placidus cusp span contains it.

Significators of a house are ranked in four steps, strongest first: planets in
the star (nakshatra) of the occupants of the house; the occupants themselves;
planets in the star of the owner (cuspal sign lord); and the owner. The raw list
is often long, so it is pruned by the sub-lord rule — a significator is fruitful
only when its own sub lord also signifies the matter.

The Cuspal Sub Lord (CSL) is decisive. For an event to be promised, the sub lord
of the relevant cusp must signify the supporting houses and must not be tied
only to the houses that deny the matter. Marriage is judged from the 7th cusp
with houses 2, 7, and 11 supporting; career from the 10th with 2, 6, 10, and 11;
childbirth from the 5th with 2, 5, and 11. When the CSL signifies the supporting
group the event is promised; the timing follows the dasha and bhukti of the
significators, with both period lords ideally signifying the same houses.

Because sub lords change within minutes, KP judgments are highly sensitive to
birth-time accuracy. When the recorded time is uncertain, treat any cusp whose
sub lord shifts inside the uncertainty window as low confidence.
