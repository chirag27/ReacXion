# Project rules for Claude Code — read this before every task
 
## How to work with me (I am NOT a programmer)
- I don't understand code or technical words. Always explain what you did in
  plain, everyday English, as if talking to a friend who has never coded. If you
  must use a technical word, explain it simply in the same sentence.
- Do ONE small step at a time. After each step, STOP. Tell me in plain words what
  you did and whether it worked. Then WAIT for me to say "continue" before doing
  anything else. Never do several steps in a row on your own.
- Always check your own work before saying it's done. If there's a way to test
  it, run the test and tell me the result in plain words.
- If you hit an error, or a choice you can't make on your own, or anything is
  unclear: STOP and ask me in plain English. Give me simple options to pick from.
  Never guess, and never make an important decision for me.
- Never delete my files, never spend money, and never install anything unusual
  without first asking me in plain words and waiting for my yes.
 
## What we're building
A personal astrology app. The computer works out the exact birth chart from a
person's birth date, time and place. Then it writes the meaning of that chart
using astrology books I will give you. We build in order: Vedic first, then KP,
then Lal Kitab. We build slowly, one small piece at a time.
 
## The most important rule about the astrology
- All exact numbers and positions in a chart (which planet is where, dates, star
  positions) must ALWAYS be worked out by the calculation part of the app. They
  must NEVER be guessed, and the part that writes the meanings must never invent
  them.
- The books are only for writing the meanings. The books never provide numbers.
- If the books don't cover a particular situation, the app should honestly say
  so, not make something up.
 
## Practical rules
- Use the trusted astronomy tool "Swiss Ephemeris" for all planet positions.
- Check the app's answers against the two AstroSage PDF charts I provided. If the
  app matches them, we know it's working.
 
## When something is finished
Tell me in plain English: what it now does, whether you tested it and what
happened, and what the next small step would be. Then wait for my go.
